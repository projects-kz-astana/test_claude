Method note for the report writer: I could not open any manufacturer page (whoop.com returned HTTP 403; garmin.com, polar.com, amazfit.com and ouraring.com were not reachable through the fetch tool), so every spec and price below comes from third-party coverage or from search-result summaries. Where a figure comes only from a search-engine summary of a page I did not open myself, I say "per search summary". Source dates are given where I could see them. Items I could not confirm as current are flagged "UNCONFIRMED". Retailer/aggregator pages (the5krunner spec database, Sensai, idevice, etc.) are secondary sources, and Sensai (a vendor blog) and the5krunner are partisan/opinionated in places.

## 1. Which devices exist and are current as of October 2026?

### Takeaway
The screenless-band category exploded in 2026: Google Fitbit Air (shipped 26 May 2026, $99.99), Garmin Cirqa (announced 21 Jul, released 24 Jul 2026, $199.99), Luna Band (retail 4 Sep 2026, $149), Speediance Strap/Strap Pro (reviewed Sep 2026, $129/$159) and Oura Ring 5 (shipped 4 Jun 2026) are all new this year, alongside the 2025 entrants Polar Loop (Sep 2025), Amazfit Helio Strap (Jun 2025) and Garmin Index Sleep Monitor (Jun 2025). Whoop's current hardware is still the May 2025 Whoop 5.0 / MG; I found no new Whoop hardware generation in 2026. Apple, Samsung, Huawei, Coros, Suunto, Withings, Wahoo and Ultrahuman (band) have NOT shipped a screenless band; those are rumor or survey stage.
